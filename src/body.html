How to play __APP_NAME__

Goal: move all 52 cards up to the four foundations, Ace to King, one suit per pile.

  • On the table, stack cards down in alternating colours (red 7 on black 8). You can move a whole face-up run at once.
  • Only a King (or a run starting with a King) can go into an empty column.
  • Tap the stock to turn 1 or 3 cards. When it runs out, tap it again to recycle the waste.
  • Face-down cards flip over when they are uncovered.

Controls

  • Tap or click a card to send it to the best spot (foundation first). Drag it if you want a different spot. Double-click works too.
  • Keys: U undo, R redo, H hint, N new deal, Space draw, A auto-finish, T theme, M sound.

Sounds

  • Cats: a meow when you deal and win, a purr on the foundation, paw taps as cards move, and a hiss for a move that isn’t allowed.
  • Aviation: engine spool-up on the deal, a radio chime on the foundation, a whoosh as cards move, a warning horn for a move that isn’t allowed, and a flyby when you win.
  • Tap 🔊 to mute. Sounds switch with the theme.

Extras

  • Unlimited undo and redo, and a smart hint that looks ahead with a solver.
  • Winnable deals: with this on, every deal has been solved by the computer first.
  • Daily challenge: everyone gets the same winnable deal each day.
  • Auto-finish appears once every card is face up and the rest is easy.
  • Stamps to collect, like Cat Nap or Barrel Roll, plus stats and streaks.
  • Players and high scores: each player on this device has their own stats. The top 10 scores for Draw 1 and Draw 3 are kept with name, time, moves and date. Tap 👤 to switch players.
  • Switch between the Cat and Aviation themes anytime. Left-handed, large-card and colour-blind options are in Settings.

Scoring: +10 to a foundation, +5 from the waste to the table, +5 for flipping a card, −15 from a foundation back to the table, and a time bonus when you win.
